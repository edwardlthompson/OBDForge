Standard F-Droid AntiFeatures: NonFreeNet may apply to the GitHub `full` flavor (optional GitHub Releases update check). The F-Droid `foss` flavor skips that auto-check and does not download Gemma.

OBDForge contains no advertising, no user tracking, and no Google Play Services or Firebase.

Network use is limited to optional adapter Wi-Fi/TCP connections, user-opened donation/source links, and (GitHub builds only) optional release update checks.

Vehicle safety (not an F-Droid AntiFeature flag): bidirectional OBD/UDS commands can modify ECU state. Safety interlocks, persona gating, expert unlock, and audit logging are documented in the app and full store description. Use at your own risk on vehicles you are authorized to service.
